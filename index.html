A comfortable home base in Kampala

Malachite Apartments is a peaceful, well-kept retreat in Kampala where comfort comes first. The apartments are spacious and thoughtfully furnished, with a private terrace and a green garden that give you room to unwind outdoors. Inside, you'll find what you need for a stay that feels like home: free Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, a fully equipped kitchenette, a washing machine, streaming services and a dining area for shared meals.

We've also taken care of the practical details. Free private parking is available on site, and full-day security keeps things safe and calm around the clock. Family rooms make the apartments a good fit for groups, and pets are welcome, so nobody has to be left behind. Entebbe International Airport is about 53 km (33 miles) away, which keeps arrivals and onward travel straightforward.
